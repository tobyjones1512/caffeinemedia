Post — what we do

Capabilities

Everything below is run in-house by the Post team. Anything the other two companies do better, we hand across the corridor rather than fake.

01

Editorial

Offline cutting rooms and online conform. Assistant editors, media management and versioning, with remote review for producers who are not in the building.

02

Colour

Grading in a colour-managed pipeline, HDR and SDR trims from a single master, and hands-on sessions with the director and DoP in the room.

03

Sound

Dialogue edit, ADR, sound design, Foley and mix. Stereo through 5.1 and Atmos, delivered with printmasters and full stem sets.

04

Motion & VFX

Titles, credit rolls, clean-up, paint-outs, screen replacements and the invisible fixes that keep a shot in the film.

05

Media conversion

Format and codec conversion, standards conversion, legacy tape and film scan ingest, subtitle and caption conformance — priced per job, turned around fast.

06

Mastering & delivery

DCP, IMF, ProRes and platform-spec masters built against the distributor's delivery schedule, QC'd before they leave, with an archive package that will still open in ten years.

Post — how it runs

Four stages, no surprises.

You always know which stage the project is in, what it costs, and what has to be true before it moves to the next one.

01

Spec

We start from the delivery requirements, not the edit. Knowing the final spec on day one is what stops a re-master in week nine.

02

Ingest

Rushes checked, checksummed and backed up in three places. You get a media report before anyone touches a timeline.

03

Finish

Conform, grade, sound and titles, scheduled so the director is only in the room for the parts that need a director in the room.

04

Deliver

Masters, versions, subtitles and QC reports against every platform's spec sheet — plus a LTO or cloud archive of the whole project.
