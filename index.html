The group — 001

A film shouldn’t have to change hands three times to reach a screen. So it doesn’t.

A three-company group built so an independent film never has to leave the building — produced, finished and released by people who answer to each other.

Each company stands on its own and takes outside work. Together they remove the handovers where independent films usually lose time, money and control.

How it runs — 003

Script to screen, without the handovers.

The same four stages every film goes through — except here the people running them share a building, a schedule and a delivery spec.

Principles — 004

Four things we won’t trade.

Written down because they are easy to hold at four projects and hard to hold at forty.

I

One roof, three doors

You can hire one company or all three. Nothing is bundled by force, and no division is allowed to hold a project hostage for another.

II

Terms in plain English

Contracts a filmmaker can read without a lawyer translating them. Rights, splits and windows stated once, in words, near the front.

III

Technically obsessive

Colour management, delivery specs and archive are not paperwork. They are the difference between a film that plays anywhere and one that plays once.

IV

Small on purpose

A short list of projects at a time, run by the people who pitched for them. Nobody hands your film to a junior after the deal closes.
