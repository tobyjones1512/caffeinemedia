Studios — what we do

Capabilities

Everything below is run in-house by the Studios team. Anything the other two companies do better, we hand across the corridor rather than fake.

01

Development

Script work, coverage and honest notes. We take a small number of projects at a time and stay on them until the draft is genuinely shootable.

02

Financing & packaging

Budgets, schedules, recoupment structures, soft money and tax credits. We put the finance plan together and attach cast, HODs and co-producers around it.

03

Physical production

Full line production and production management. Crewing, scheduling, insurance, locations, compliance and cost reporting through to wrap.

04

Documentary

Long-lead factual and observational work, structured so a project can start shooting before it is fully financed without losing control of it.

05

Shorts & proof of concept

A deliberately fast route for directors who need a calling card, or for a feature that needs to prove its tone before anyone will fund it.

06

Service production

Ground support for productions shooting in the UK — crew, kit, locations and back-office, run under our books or yours.

Studios — how it runs

Four stages, no surprises.

You always know which stage the project is in, what it costs, and what has to be true before it moves to the next one.

01

Read

You send the script or the treatment. You get a real response in two weeks — a yes, a no, or the two things standing between it and a yes.

02

Shape

Development against a fixed number of drafts and a fixed fee, so the project cannot quietly live in development for three years.

03

Finance

Budget, schedule and finance plan built together. We are honest early about what the film can cost and still recoup.

04

Shoot

Full production. Post is already booked with Caffeine Post before the first day of principal photography.
