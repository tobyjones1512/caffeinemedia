Films — what we do

Capabilities

Everything below is run in-house by the Films team. Anything the other two companies do better, we hand across the corridor rather than fake.

01

Acquisitions

Completed features, festival premieres and works in progress. We watch everything that comes in and we tell you what we think, even when the answer is no.

02

Festival strategy

A submission plan built around one honest assessment of where the film can realistically premiere, and what a premiere there is worth.

03

Theatrical

Targeted theatrical and event releases — the cities, screens and dates where a specific film can hold, rather than a wide run that quietly loses money.

04

Digital & VOD

Transactional, subscription and ad-supported placement, plus the platform relationships and metadata work that decides whether anyone ever sees the artwork.

05

Sales & licensing

International sales, territory licensing, broadcast and airline, and the rights tracking that keeps every window straight.

06

Campaign

Positioning, key art, trailers, press and paid media, produced in-house with Caffeine Post so the assets arrive on time and on spec.

Films — how it runs

Four stages, no surprises.

You always know which stage the project is in, what it costs, and what has to be true before it moves to the next one.

01

Watch

You send a link. We watch the whole thing and come back within two weeks with a genuine opinion and a view on the market.

02

Position

Who this film is for, where they already are, and what it costs to reach them. Written down before any money is committed.

03

Release

Festivals, theatrical, digital and international, sequenced into a single window plan — not three teams working against each other.

04

Report

Transparent quarterly statements with the line items shown. You can see what was spent, what came back and what is still outstanding.
